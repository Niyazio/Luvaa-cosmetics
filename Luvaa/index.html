How to Use Luvaa Mixing Whitening Cream

Follow these essential steps for safe application and the best glowing results.

  • Wash your face using soap or face wash and dry your face completely before applying the cream.
  • Apply only a small amount of cream at night before sleeping.
  • Do not apply near the eyes, inside eyes, or sensitive areas.
  • Do not expose your face to sunlight while the cream is applied.
  • Keep the product away from children.
  • Use continuously until visible results appear.
  • After your face becomes brighter, use the cream with a 2-day gap.
